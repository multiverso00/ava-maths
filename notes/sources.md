## What the rules now use, and where it comes from

- Stellenwerttafel headings are H, Z, E. The rules say Hunderter, Zehner, Einer in words. The grid keeps H, T, O because the English test uses those letters.
- Written methods start at the ones. The Planungshilfe's spoken form is "Ich beginne bei den Einern". The column sheet says "Du beginnst bei den Einern."
- Addition with carrying: "Schreibe 4 Einer und ‚merke 1' an der Zehnerstelle", "die gemerkte 1", "Merkzahl". The carried 1 is written between the second number and the result line. The site does not draw it. It only names it.
- Subtraction: Abziehverfahren and Ergänzungsverfahren (also called Auffüllen) are both allowed. The Planungshilfe says a class does not have to be taught both. In both of its worked examples the higher place is broken up first ("entbündele ich den Hunderter in 10 Zehner"). The site checks only the result, so either method passes. The rules use "entbündeln", which fits both.
- The Planungshilfe flags the typical mistake of subtracting the smaller digit from the larger one column by column (235 − 153, "30 − 50 = 20?"). That is the smaller-from-larger note.
- Mental sums with 1s, 10s or 100s use the Planungshilfe's question "Welche Stelle verändert sich? Welche Stellen bleiben gleich?" No written-column rule is given for these.
- Halbschriftlich strategies: schrittweise, stellenweise, Hilfsaufgabe, Ergänzen, shown on the Rechenstrich. Crossing notes use "first up to the round ten (or hundred), then the rest", the step-by-step route with round intermediate results in the PIKAS material.
- Überschlag: the Planungshilfe accepts more than one sensible estimate (376 + 248 as 380 + 250, 400 + 200, or 370 + 240). Rounding is one option among several. When a question asks for hundreds, the site also accepts rounding to tens.
- Checking: the Planungshilfe says "Umkehroperation". PIKAS uses "Umkehraufgabe" with children. The rules say "Umkehraufgabe".
- Dienes material: Hunderterplatten, Zehnerstangen, Einerwürfel.

## Still uncertain

- Textbook. Berlin has no central approval list, and each school chooses its own. Lehrwerke with Berlin editions or Berlin plans include Welt der Zahl (Westermann, Ausgabe 2022), Denken und Rechnen (Westermann, Stoffverteilungsplan Berlin/Brandenburg) and Das Zahlenbuch (Klett; for example, the Clemens-Brentano-Grundschule in Steglitz uses it). Her class's book is not known, so the rules follow the Rahmenlehrplan.
  https://www.bildungstor.de/schule/unterricht/lernmittel/
- "Hunderterübergang". No official definition was found. The wording follows "Zehnerübergang", plus "Zum Hunderter hin / Vom Hunderter weg" from the Denken und Rechnen plan.
- "Probe". It is common in German classrooms, but the Planungshilfe does not use it. The site does not use it either.
